You don't need to win most of your trades. You need to win the RIGHT trades.

```

Trade 2: -45 pips (moved stop loss, hoped for reversal)
Trade 3: +20 pips (closed early again)
Trade 4: -50 pips (revenge trade after loss)
Trade 5: +10 pips (scalped to "make back" losses)
Trade 6: -60 pips (big loss, no stop loss)
Trade 7: +12 pips
Total: -98 pips (5 wins, 2 losses = 71% win rate but LOSING money)

TRADER B (Disciplined — Follows the system):
Trade 1: -20 pips (proper SL hit, accepted the loss)
Trade 2: SKIPPED (no valid setup, sat on hands)
Trade 3: -20 pips (proper SL hit again)

Trade 5: +60 pips (1:3 R:R, let it run to target)
Trade 6: SKIPPED (setup was B grade, waited)
Trade 7: +40 pips (1:2 R:R, hit target)
Total: +60 pips (2 wins, 2 losses = 50% win rate but PROFITABLE)
```

The difference: Trader B has a SYSTEM and follows it. Trader A has knowledge but no discipline.

## The 10 Rules of the Gatekeeper

Print these out. Tape them to your monitor. These are non-negotiable.

### Rule 1: The Sequence Must Complete
No step can be skipped. H4 bias → H1 sweep + BoS → M15 zone → M5 trigger. If step 2 isn't there, you don't look at step 3. Period.

**What this feels like**: "I can see a great FVG on M15 but there's no sweep on H1..." You will WANT to take the trade. You won't. Your Gatekeeper app will show SKIP. Trust it.

### Rule 2: Maximum 3 Trades Per Day
After 3 trades, you're done for the day regardless of outcome. Why?

- Trade 1-3: Planned, calculated, emotional baseline is stable
- Trade 4+: Decisions degrade. If you won, you're overconfident. If you lost, you're emotional

**Exception**: Zero exceptions. Even if the "perfect" setup appears after your 3rd trade, you walk away.

### Rule 3: Maximum 2 Losses Per Day — Then You're Blocked
If you lose 2 trades in a day, you stop trading for the rest of the day. Your Gatekeeper app enforces this.

**Why**: After 2 losses, your brain shifts from "following the plan" to "making back what I lost." This emotional state makes you see setups that aren't there. The third trade after two losses has the lowest win rate of any trade you'll ever take.

### Rule 4: Risk Exactly 1% Per Trade (2% Maximum)
Never adjust based on "how confident" you feel.

```
"I'm really confident about this one, let me risk 3%"
                    ↓
        This is how accounts blow up.
        
Confidence is an emotion, not an edge.
The edge is in the SYSTEM, not your feelings.
```

- 1% risk: You can lose 10 trades in a row and still have 90% of your account
- 2% risk: You can lose 10 in a row and still have 82%
- 5% risk: 10 losses = 60% of account gone, psychologically destroyed

### Rule 5: Stop Loss Is Sacred — Never Move It Further Away
You can TIGHTEN your stop (move it toward breakeven). You can NEVER widen it.

**What happens when you widen stops**: You turn a planned 1:2 risk trade into an unplanned 1:0.5 disaster. One widened stop can erase 5 winning trades.

**Your Gatekeeper rule**: Once the app calculates SL, that's the SL. If you move it wider, you're no longer following the system.

### Rule 6: Take Profit at the Target — Don't Get Greedy
When price hits your TP level, close the trade. Don't think "maybe it'll go further."

**Exception**: You can trail your stop to lock in profit once you're at 1:1 or better. But never remove your TP entirely.

```
Bad habit:
TP was at 1.0950 → Price reaches 1.0945 → "Almost there, let me extend TP to 1.0980"
→ Price reverses → You end up at breakeven or a loss

Good habit:
TP was at 1.0950 → Price reaches 1.0945 → You wait
→ Price hits 1.0950 → CLOSED → Done → Walk away
```

### Rule 7: No Trading Outside Kill Zones
London Open (07:00-10:00 GMT), NY Open (12:00-15:00 GMT), London Close (15:00-17:00 GMT). That's it.

**The temptation**: "It's 10 PM and I see a perfect setup..." It's not perfect. Low volume = unpredictable moves. The setup will be there tomorrow during a kill zone, or it wasn't real.

### Rule 8: No Trading During Red News Events
NFP, CPI, FOMC, ECB rate decisions, employment data — check the economic calendar BEFORE your trading session.

**Rule**: No new entries within 30 minutes before or 15 minutes after a red-flag news event. If you're already in a trade, either close it before the event or accept the risk.

**Why**: During news, price can gap 50-100 pips in seconds. Your stop loss might not execute at your price (slippage). The market doesn't follow SMC during news — it follows data.

### Rule 9: If You're Emotional, You Don't Trade
Before every session, check yourself:

```
Am I angry about a previous loss?          → Don't trade
Am I excited about a previous win?         → Don't trade  
Am I stressed about money?                 → Don't trade
Am I trading to "prove something"?         → Don't trade
Am I bored and looking for action?         → Don't trade
Am I calm and following my plan?           → Trade
```

Your Gatekeeper app has an emotional risk assessment. If it says HIGH, it means STOP.

### Rule 10: Journal Every Trade — Win or Lose
After each trade (including SKIPs and WAITs), document:
- What the Gatekeeper said (the full analysis)
- Whether you followed it
- What happened
- What you learned

This journal is your REAL education. After 50 trades, patterns emerge — not chart patterns, but patterns in YOUR behavior.

## The Emotional Traps — Know Your Enemy

### Trap 1: Revenge Trading
**What it is**: Taking a trade immediately after a loss to "make it back"
**How it feels**: Urgent. Like you NEED to trade right now.
**Reality**: You're not seeing a setup. You're seeing what you want to see.
**The fix**: Rule 3 (2 losses = done for the day). Non-negotiable.

### Trap 2: FOMO (Fear of Missing Out)
**What it is**: Entering a trade because price is moving and you're not in it
**How it feels**: "It's going without me! I need to get in NOW!"
**Reality**: If you missed the entry zone, the R:R is already bad. You're chasing.
**The fix**: If your Gatekeeper says MISSED, it means MISSED. The market will give you another setup.

### Trap 3: Overconfidence After Wins
**What it is**: Increasing lot size or taking lower-grade setups after a winning streak
**How it feels**: "I'm on fire. I can't lose right now."
**Reality**: The market doesn't know or care that you won 5 in a row. The 6th trade has the same probability as any other.
**The fix**: Same 1% risk. Same grade requirements. Every single trade.

### Trap 4: Moving Your Stop Loss
**What it is**: Widening your SL because "it just needs a little more room"
**How it feels**: Rational in the moment. "It's going to turn around."
**Reality**: You're hoping, not trading. The stop was placed at that level for a reason.
**The fix**: The SL is set before entry and never touched (except to tighten).

### Trap 5: Analysis Paralysis
**What it is**: Over-analyzing until you talk yourself out of every trade
**How it feels**: "I need to check one more timeframe... one more indicator..."
**Reality**: If the 5-step sequence confirms, the trade is valid. More analysis won't increase your edge.
**The fix**: Trust the system. If it's A grade on the Gatekeeper, take it. If it's not, skip it. No gray area.

### Trap 6: Indicator Addiction
**What it is**: Adding RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, Stochastic to "confirm" your SMC setup
**How it feels**: "More confirmation = safer trade"
**Reality**: Every indicator you add creates another potential reason to SKIP a valid setup. Indicators lag — they tell you what already happened, not what will happen.
**The fix**: Your only tools are price action + 20 EMA. That's the system. Adding indicators breaks it.

## The Drawdown Protocol

Drawdown = how far your account has fallen from its peak.

```
Peak balance: $10,000
Current balance: $9,500
Drawdown: 5%

Drawdown levels and responses:

0-3%:    Normal. Keep trading the system.
3-5%:    Caution. Review last 5 trades for pattern violations.
         Reduce max trades per day to 2.
5-7%:    Serious. Stop trading for 24 hours.
         Review ALL trades from the drawdown period.
         Identify which rules you broke.
7-10%:   Critical. Stop trading for 1 week.
         Paper trade only. Re-read this document.
         Only resume live trading after 10 consecutive
         paper trades following the system.
10%+:    Emergency. Stop trading indefinitely.
         Your system isn't working OR you're not following it.
         Go back to paper trading for 30 days minimum.
```

**Why this matters**: Drawdown isn't just about money — it's about psychology. At 10% drawdown, you need 11% gain to break even. At 20%, you need 25%. At 50%, you need 100%. Small losses compound. Protecting your capital IS the strategy.

## The Weekly Review Process

Every weekend (Saturday or Sunday), spend 30 minutes reviewing:

### 1. Trade Log
- How many trades did you take this week?
- How many did the Gatekeeper approve as A/A+ grade?
- How many times did you override the Gatekeeper?
- What was your win rate on approved trades vs overrides?

### 2. Rule Violations
- Did you trade outside kill zones?
- Did you trade after 2 losses in a day?
- Did you move a stop loss?
- Did you take a C-grade trade?
- Did you trade during news?

### 3. Patterns in Your Behavior
After a month of reviews, you'll notice things like:
- "I always overtrade on Mondays"
- "I revenge trade after losses on GBPUSD but not EURUSD"
- "My best trades are during London open, my worst during NY afternoon"
- "I keep entering too early before M5 confirmation"

These patterns are GOLD. They tell you exactly where your discipline breaks down.

### 4. System Performance
- Is the SMC sequence identifying valid setups?
- Are A-grade setups winning at a good rate?
- Are SKIP verdicts correctly avoiding losing trades?

If A-grade setups are consistently losing, the issue might be with chart reading (noisy MT5 template), not the system.

## The Mental Model: You Are the Casino

Casinos don't win every hand. They win over thousands of hands because the MATH is in their favor.

```
Casino edge in blackjack: ~1-2% per hand
Your edge with SMC (properly executed): ~5-15% per trade

The casino doesn't:
- Double down after a losing hand out of emotion
- Skip a round because they "feel" unlucky  
- Change the rules of blackjack because they lost 3 in a row
- Let players negotiate the house rules

You shouldn't either.

Your rules = the house rules
Your Gatekeeper = the dealer who enforces them
Your discipline = what makes the math work over 100+ trades
```

One trade means nothing. Ten trades mean very little. One hundred trades following the system — that's where you find out if you're profitable.
